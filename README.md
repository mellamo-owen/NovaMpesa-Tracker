🚀 Welcome to NovaMpesa Tracker (v1.0)
Developed by Novanest Labs

What it is: A 100% offline, privacy-first Android app that automatically tracks your M-Pesa spending. It reads your Safaricom M-Pesa SMS, helps you categorize your expenses instantly, and gives you deep insights into your spending habits without needing an internet connection.

🛠️ How to Install & Setup (Crucial Steps)
Because this app is installed directly (not from the Play Store), Android will try to block it for your safety. Please follow these steps carefully:

Install: If your phone warns you about "unknown sources", tap Allow or Settings > Allow from this source.

Bypass Restricted Settings:

Go to Settings > Apps > NovaMpesa Tracker.

Tap the Three Dots (⋮) in the top right.

Tap "Allow restricted settings".

Allow Permissions:

Still in the App Settings, tap Permissions.

Allow SMS and Notifications.

Battery Optimization:

Go to Settings > Apps > NovaMpesa Tracker > Battery.

Select Unrestricted. (If you skip this, your phone will sleep the app in the background and it won't detect your M-Pesa messages).

✨ The Innovations (What makes this app special)
M-Pesa Runway: Tells you exactly how many days your current M-Pesa balance will last based on your daily spending rate.

Automatic Categorization: When an M-Pesa message arrives, a notification pops up. You just tap Food, Transport, or More... to save it instantly.

Money In / Money Out: It automatically detects when you receive money, deposit cash at an M-Pesa shop, or send money out. It keeps your totals strictly accurate.

The Leakage Tracker: Shows you how much you are spending on small things (under Ksh 200) like snacks and boda rides.

External Deposits & Loans: Keeps money sent to M-Shwari, banks, or friends (loans) separate from your daily expenses.

Downloadable PDF Reports: Generates a professional PDF report with charts and your transaction history at the end of the month.

🖱️ What Every Button Does (The App Walkthrough)
1. The Home Screen
Spent This Week/Month: Your total outgoing expenses (including transaction fees).

Runway: How many days your money will last.

Deposits: Money moved to banks/M-Shwari.

Owed to You: Money you lent to friends.

Bar Chart: A visual breakdown of your daily spending.

Palette Icon (Top Right): Changes the app's color theme (Lavender, Sky Blue, or Pink).

2. The Expenses Tab (Transactions)
Search Bar: Type a name or M-Pesa code to find a specific transaction.

Filter Pills: Tap All, Money out, or Money in to filter the list.

Tap Any Transaction: Opens a menu to:

Categorize it (Food, Transport, etc.).

Mark as External Deposit.

Mark as Loan.

Delete it.

The + Button (Bottom Right): Manually add a cash expense.

3. The Budgets Tab
Shows you exactly how much you spent this week in each of the 8 categories (Food, Transport, Giving, Clothes, Shoes, Cosmetics, Hair & Nails, Other).

4. The Analytics Tab
Total Spent: Your total expenses for the month.

Transaction Costs: The total fees Safaricom charged you.

The Chart: Visual spending breakdown.

Excel Table: A scrollable list of your recent transactions.

Download PDF: Tap this at the bottom to save a full PDF report (Charts + Table) directly to your phone's Downloads folder.

🔔 How the Smart Notifications Work
When you pay via M-Pesa, the app pushes a notification saying: "Ksh 150 sent to NAIVAS".

Tap Food or Transport to save it instantly.

Tap External Deposit if you deposited it to a bank/Savings.

Tap More... to pick one of the other 8 categories.
